About Me

Building the Web, One Project at a Time

I'm a developer focused on creating thoughtful digital products that bring together design, technology, and functionality. Over the past year, I've worked across a range of projects, from e-commerce and creative platforms to professional websites, portfolio systems, and content management solutions.

My experience spans frontend development, UI/UX, responsive web design, content management, authentication, and deployment. I choose tools based on what each project needs, with a focus on building interfaces that are clear, reliable, and purposeful.

I enjoy taking an idea from its first structure all the way to something people can actually use, solving the technical problems that come up along the way. Each project has taught me not just how to build a website, but how the pieces of a digital product fit together.
