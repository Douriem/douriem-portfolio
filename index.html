About Me

Building the Web, One Project at a Time

I'm a developer focused on creating thoughtful digital products that bring together design, technology, and functionality. Over the past year, I've worked across a range of projects, from e-commerce and creative platforms to professional websites, portfolio systems, and content management solutions. My experience spans frontend development, UI/UX, responsive web design, content management, authentication, deployment, and the integration of modern web technologies. I work across different tools and technologies depending on what a project requires, with a focus on building interfaces that are clear, reliable, and purposeful. I enjoy taking an idea from its initial structure through development and refinement, solving the technical problems that appear along the way, and turning it into something people can actually use. Each project has pushed me to understand not just how to build a website, but how the different pieces of a digital product work together.
